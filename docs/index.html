The Atelier

One material, worked by hand

Atelier Bismuth began on Etsy with a devotion to a single, ancient material: travertine. Each piece is cut from solid stone and finished by hand — chiselled, honed, or left raw — so the grain of the earth stays visible in your home.

Every measurement is published, so the piece you imagine is the piece that arrives.
